# Replies and fee offers

Read the full newest message and the thread before you act. Then use this table.

## What to do with each reply

| Newest message | Action | State |
|---|---|---|
| Interested, or a factual question | Answer from facts that the person confirmed. Draft the answer if replies are not approved. | Replied |
| A fee, a sponsorship, a featured spot, or a link trade | Record the offer. Prepare the decision below. Ask for missing terms only if the person allowed it. | Needs your decision |
| A test account, a call, a logo, or other assets | State the exact request. Do not grant access or invent an asset. | Needs your decision |
| No | Stop contact. | Declined |
| Unsubscribe, or do not contact | Stop contact with the publisher. Never reply. | Do not contact |
| Bounce | Record it. Do not try another address at that publisher. | Do not contact |
| Out of office | Wait until the later of the return date and the normal due date. | No change |
| "Your entry is live" | Read the page. Confirm the business, the entry, and the promised terms. | Won, after you see the entry |
| Receipt, ticket number, or other automatic message | Keep any useful detail. Do not count it as a reply. | No change |

A message with a ticket number, or one that calls itself automatic, is automatic. Read the message headers when you can: `Auto-Submitted: auto-replied` proves it. A fee quote can also come from an automatic system. Keep the price, and do not count it as a person's interest.

## The fee decision

Some publishers answer a pitch with a price. That is the person's decision, not the agent's. Bring them one block for each offer:

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Publisher and article | The exact article address, not the site. |
| What the fee buys | A listing, a review, or a featured position. A fee for a review is not a fee for inclusion. Ask which one it is. |
| Amount | The amount, the currency, and whether it is one payment or a repeated charge. |
| Term | How long the entry stays, and the renewal terms. |
| Position and link | The promised position and link, as the publisher wrote them. |
| Disclosure | How the page marks paid entries. |
| Evidence | The answers that cite this exact page, the number of AI apps and questions, and the dates. Use the page's counts, not the totals of the whole site. |
| Missing terms | Each term that the publisher did not state. |
| Recommendation | Yes, no, or ask for terms, with the reason in one sentence. For a link trade, give the terms and no recommendation. |

Rules for the recommendation:

- Compare the price with how often AI apps cite that exact page. A cheap listing on a page that no answer cites buys nothing that you can measure.
- A higher position on the list is not proven to earn more mentions. Do not pay more for position on that claim.
- Prefer an offer with a clear scope and a short time to publication.
- A declined offer stays declined. Do not open it again through another contact.
- With no Frontmost AI project, write "No citation data" in Evidence. Give the terms, and say that you cannot compare the price with citations.

## After the person decides

- Payment is the person's action. Match the invoice to the offer first: the payee, the amount, the currency, the item, and the invoice number.
- A paid invoice is not a listing. Check the live page as a separate step.
- If the entry is live and a promised link or position is missing, record both facts. Tell the person before any message to the publisher.

## Counting replies

- Count each publisher once.
- The denominator is the publishers with a confirmed send or a confirmed form.
- A real reply addresses the pitch: terms, a question, a no, or a hand-over to an editor.
- Automatic messages, receipts, and bounces are not real replies. Report bounces apart.
- Response rate is not acceptance, publication, or sales. Report each of those with its own count.
