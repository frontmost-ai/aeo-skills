# Example: `tactic-check`

This is the result of one run on November 13, 2026. The business and the proposal are invented. The rules are real. Open each link before you rely on a rule, because rules change.

## The request

```text
Check this agency proposal for my plumbing business in Austin.

GEO Growth Package - $2,400/month
1. llms.txt file creation and "AI schema" markup - $400 setup
2. Guaranteed ChatGPT mentions within 60 days
3. 40 city landing pages generated with AI, one per city we serve
4. 25 five-star Google reviews per month from our reviewer network
5. Reddit seeding: 15 posts per month from aged accounts
6. Submission to 300 directories for AI citations
7. Add "Best Plumber Austin" to your Google Business Profile name
8. Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt and fix pages that block snippets
```

## The result

**Do not sign this package as written.** Only item 8 is clearly reasonable. Items 4, 5, and 7 are against a platform rule or a law. Item 3 is against a rule unless each page has real, different local facts.

I opened the official pages today, November 13, 2026. The Reddit page did not load, so I mark it "not checked today."

| # | Tactic | Result | Reason | Source | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | llms.txt and "AI schema", $400 setup | Unproven | Google: "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup to appear in these features." Also: "There's also no special schema.org structured data that you need to add." | [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) | Add ordinary `LocalBusiness` or `Plumber` structured data that matches your page text. Do not pay $400 for it. |
| 2 | Guaranteed ChatGPT mentions in 60 days | Unproven | Nobody outside OpenAI controls ChatGPT answers. No public method is proven to earn a mention. | none | Ask the same plumbing questions many times before and after, and count the mentions. |
| 3 | 40 AI city pages | **Against a rule** if the pages are near-copies. Reasonable if each page has real local facts. | Google names doorway pages: "pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page". Google also names scaled content abuse: "large amounts of unoriginal content that provides little to no value to users, no matter how it's created". | [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | Make pages only for the cities where you have real jobs, photos, or reviews. Expect fewer than 40 pages. |
| 4 | 25 five-star reviews a month from a "reviewer network" | **Against a rule and a United States law** | The FTC rule has been in effect since October 21, 2024. Its sections include "Fake or False Consumer Reviews" and "Buying Positive or Negative Consumer Reviews". Google forbids content "not based on a real experience". | [FTC rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers), [Google Maps policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114) | After each job, ask each customer for an honest Google review. Give no reward. Do not ask only the happy customers. |
| 5 | 15 Reddit posts a month from aged accounts | **Against a rule** | Reddit forbids content manipulation and spam (not checked today: the page did not load). In the United States, the FTC's guides say that a paid writer discloses the connection to your business. | [Reddit Rules](https://redditinc.com/policies/reddit-rules), [FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) | Reply in local threads as yourself, with your role stated, where the community's rules allow it. |
| 6 | 300 directories "for AI citations" | Unproven | No evidence shows that the number of listings earns AI mentions. If the directories sell links, Google names "Buying or selling links for ranking purposes" as link spam. | [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | Claim and correct the few listings where your buyers look. |
| 7 | Add "Best Plumber Austin" to the Google Business Profile name | **Against a rule** | "Your name should reflect your business's real-world name". The page says that a name "must not include" marketing taglines, service or product information, or location information. | [Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) | Keep your real business name. Put services and areas in the categories and the service-area fields. |
| 8 | Allow `OAI-SearchBot` and fix pages that block snippets | Reasonable | OpenAI: sites that opt out "will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers". Google: a page "must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet". | [OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) | Keep this item. It is a one-time job, not a monthly cost. |

**1. The tactics against a rule, with the risk of each one (United States rules):**

- **Item 4, reviews.** This risk is the largest. The FTC page says: "The rule authorizes courts to impose civil penalties for knowing violations". Google says that fake engagement "is not allowed and will be removed".
- **Item 7, profile name.** Google says that unnecessary information in the name "could result in the suspension of your Business Profile".
- **Item 5, Reddit.** Reddit can ban the accounts. This is my inference, because the page did not load.
- **Item 3, city pages.** Google: "Sites that violate our policies may rank lower in results or not appear in results at all."

**2. The cost of the unproven tactics.** The only priced line is the $400 setup for item 1. The $2,400 a month has no price for each line. I cannot give the cost of items 2 and 6 until the agency splits the price.

**3. Questions for the agency:**

- "Give me a price for each of the 8 lines."
- "Item 2: How do you measure a mention? Which questions, how many answers for each question, which dates? What do I get back if the guarantee fails?"
- "Item 4: Who are the people in your reviewer network? Did each one hire us?"
- "Item 5: Who owns the Reddit accounts? Does each post say that we paid for it?"
- "Item 6: Which 300 directories? Does one of them sell links? Who owns the listings after the contract ends?"

This is not legal advice. For the FTC risk, ask a lawyer. The decision is yours.
